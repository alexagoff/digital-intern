# General
[The Open Archival Information System Reference Model: Introductory Guide (2nd Edition)](http://dx.doi.org/10.7207/twr14-02)<br>
(DPC, 2014) 37pp<br>
Get familiar with the OAIS model and jargon.

[Checking Your Digital Content](https://www.digitalpreservation.gov/documents/NDSA-Fixity-Guidance-Report-final100214.pdf)<br>
(NDSA, 2013) 8pp<br>
Overview of fixity checking.

[A Brief History of Digital Preservation](https://scholarworks.umt.edu/ml_pubs/31/)<br>
(Baucom, 2019) 18pp

[AIMS Born-Digital Collections: An Inter-Institutional Model for Stewardship](https://urldefense.com/v3/__https://matienzo.org/storage/2012/2012-Jan-AIMS-WhitePaper.pdf__;!!C5qS4YX3!GMK786HIpvgIeryGxhCBwprgpS3MWCCK5iU_GUPrw-34P6adn-b_8c7d4ILCYUZCww98miBEW_mODMaB_w$)<br>
(various authors, 2012) 195pp<br>
Long, some can be skimmed. Good case studies and templates. 

[OSSArcFlow Guide to Documenting Born-Digital Archival Workflows](https://educopia.org/research-project/ossarcflow/)<br>
(IMLS, 2020) 61pp

[Digital Preservation Handbook](https://www.dpconline.org/handbook)<br>
(DPC, 2015) Review table of contents for future reference needs.

[Digital Forensics and Born-Digital Content in Cultural Heritage Collections](https://www.clir.org/pubs/reports/pub149/)<br>
(CLIR, 2010) 62pp<br>
An overview of the terminology and application of digital forensics to archival collections. Skim.
